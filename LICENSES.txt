Clear Skies: licenses and credits
=================================

Clear Skies is a mod for Savage Wind Recomp. It adds the Clarity look from Cemu's Breath of the Wild graphic
pack. It is a fan project, not made by or affiliated with the Savage Wind Recomp team, the Cemu team or Nintendo.

2. Clarity shader and presets (Cemu graphic packs)
--------------------------------------------------
Source:  https://github.com/cemu-project/cemu_graphic_packs
         src/BreathOfTheWild/Enhancements/37040a485a29d54e_00000000000003c9_ps.txt and rules.txt
         (copied unchanged into third_party/cemu/, from commit 7e476164072e5978f62fd727cb013d474664e793)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 asks for nothing in return, but the credit belongs to the people who made it:
  Clarity GFX: Jamie (main code) and Kiri (code and ReShade logic), with Crementif.
  Presets: Serfrost (Recommended, Dim Display, Bright Display), Bruz, CellHunter, Jamie, t3nka (Neutral+),
  The Complaining Gamer, Visk (ViskClarity), Silentverge (Survival of the Wild, Warmer and Colder),
  Godot (Mexican), Hexaae (Clear), MaranBr, YeMaoWuXin (Bright colors, Vivid and clear), Cobalt; the Contrasty
  and Filmic presets and the User-Defined starting values come with the pack without a named creator.
  Several passes are ports of ReShade effects (LumaSharpen, Technicolor, Technicolor 2, Levels, Lift Gamma Gain,
  Vibrance, FilmicPass, DPX, Curves) as they appear in the pack.

What Clear Skies changes: the presets and functions are read from those files by tools/gen_presets.py; the
shader's inputs are renamed for Savage Wind, a pixel's size comes from the scene texture instead of Cemu's
resolution scale, the HDR scene is clamped at 10000 as the game's own shader does, the result is clamped to
[0, 1], and Tone_map 9 runs ACES (the pack tests a misspelt name there, so it does nothing in Cemu).
